## Session: Phase 1 complete — Lead adjudication of C1–C7 and Ai's 5 open questions

**All specialist output received. Phase 1 CLOSED.**

### Rulings on Yan's C1–C7 (finance)
- C1 **ACCEPT** — money unit is **integer sen** everywhere. Already implemented in `money.ts`.
- C2 **ACCEPT** — zero-weight/no-payment pool ⇒ refund nothing + warn. Cannot refund uncollected money.
- C3 **ACCEPT** — `BUFFER_MINUTES` default **0**, configurable. Implemented (window params in `conflict.ts`).
- C4 **ACCEPT** — conflict ⇒ **WARN + suggest alternative**, never hard-block. Strict mode opt-in.
  Rationale: hard-blocking makes the product unusable when a class is merely adjacent, and the
  founder may legitimately choose to act. Human stays in control. This also aligns with the
  design spec's disabled-CTA rule (disabled only while an action REMAINS on a blocked slot).
- C5 **ACCEPT** — post-approval supplier update ⇒ **advisory only**, never auto-reverse executed refunds.
- C6 **ACCEPT (Lead decision of record)** — supplier recovery `SM` never reduces customer refund `CP`.
  Founder is merchant of record; customers are refunded what they paid.
- C7 **ACCEPT** — half-up, and by design no customer amount comes from a half-sen division.

### Rulings on Ai's 5 open questions (design)
1. **Downstream of approval**: approve **prepares + stages**; it does NOT move money. MVP has no
   live payment gateway (none configured). Messages are staged, not sent. So the disclaimer copy
   "does not move money until you confirm again" is CORRECT and must stay. Execution is recorded
   as an auditable `executed` transition with no external gateway call in the MVP.
2. **Fixture total**: use **`RM 428.40`** (the row sum). Ai's table is internally consistent.
   Adopt Ai's 6-customer Sarawak fixture as the canonical demo dataset.
3. **Academic calendar source**: **user-entered, persisted to the DB** (master prompt says
   "Study Protection using manually entered class and study commitments"). Seed a default set for
   the demo path, but the entry path is real and writes to `commitments`.
4. **Message channel**: confirmed — **staged for approval**, no per-message send button. §5.4 holds.
5. **Dark mode**: stays **out of scope** for Phase 1.

`hasSlot?: boolean` to `PlanStep`. "Review refunds" is a founder in-app action with

scheduled business actions are checked. This preserved the intended two-beat demo
(one blocked action, one safe action) rather than weakening the test.

**Design decision of record**: `PlanStep.hasSlot` distinguishes founder in-app actions
from scheduled business actions. Do not reintroduce a slot on review steps.
